1. Quotes and prices

  • Prices on this site are flat and include parts, unless a listing says otherwise.

2. Before you hand over your device

  • Back up anything you care about. I don't access your data, but repairs can sometimes reset a device.
  • Remove discs, cases, cards and accessories you don't need repaired.
  • Tell me about any earlier repairs, drops or liquid spills.

3. Repairs I don't take

  • Water or liquid damage. Corrosion keeps spreading after a device dries, so repairs rarely last.
  • Devices that are banned, locked to someone else's account, or reported stolen.
  • Services marked "Coming soon", like phone repair and soldering or board repair (HDMI and charging ports). I don't take these jobs until they're listed with a price.
  • Any job I don't believe I can do reliably. I'll tell you upfront.

4. If I can't fix it

You don't pay for the repair. Your device comes back as you gave it to me. I take photos when I receive a device so we both know its condition.

5. Payment

Cash or Zelle, after the repair is done and you've seen it working. Opening offers like 20% off are limited to my first 5 repair customers, one per customer, and can't be combined with other offers. Leaving a review is never required to get the discount, and you can leave any review you want.

6. 30-day guarantee

If the same problem I fixed comes back within 30 days, I'll fix it again for free.

The guarantee doesn't cover:

  • New problems unrelated to my repair
  • Drops, cracks, liquid or other damage after pickup
  • Devices opened or repaired by someone else after me
  • Parts I didn't replace, like the other stick on a controller where I replaced one

Please pick up your device within 30 days after I tell you it's ready. I'll keep reaching out by text and email until you do.
