Accessibility policy

Lami Tech Repair is committed to making its website and services usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control, magnification or other assistive technology. Accessibility is checked every time the site is changed, and problems people report are treated as a priority.

Conformance status

As of November 18, 2026, the pages listed below conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, published by the W3C at https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/.

  • Pages covered: https://lamitechrepair.pages.dev/ (home page, including the search bar, device categories and the "What needs fixing?" tool), /terms.html, /privacy.html and /accessibility.html
  • Technologies this conformance relies on: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and WAI-ARIA
  • How it was checked: a self-evaluation by the site owner using the axe-core automated testing engine for WCAG 2.2 A and AA, plus manual checks: keyboard-only use, visible focus, focus not hidden by the sticky menu or phone bar, target sizes, reflow at 320 pixels wide, 200% text size, increased text spacing, reduced motion and high-contrast modes, the Menu button (open, close with Esc, focus returns) and the top bar that fades on scroll
  • Not covered: websites this site links to, such as Google, Facebook, Yelp and Apple Maps, and the phone, text and email apps on your own device

What's built in

  • Strong color contrast, and information is never shown by color alone.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Form choices have labels and instructions, and clear error messages appear if something is missing.
  • Pop-up windows can be closed with the Esc key, and focus returns to where you were.
  • Images and icons have text descriptions, or are hidden from screen readers when they're only decoration.
  • The moving background at the top can be paused, and it stays still if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Buttons and links are large enough to tap, and the page works with larger text, extra spacing and narrow screens.
  • The site adjusts for high contrast modes, including Windows Contrast Themes.
  • On smaller screens, a Menu button lists every section. It opens and closes with a keyboard, and Esc closes it.
  • The top bar fades out when you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up, hover, tap the top of the screen or move keyboard focus to it. If your device is set to reduce motion or increase contrast, it never fades.
  • Prices are in a real table with column headers, so screen readers read each price with its repair.

Alternative formats and help in person

I can send any page of this site, a quote, or a receipt in large print, as plain text, or read it to you over the phone. If you have a disability and need a repair handled differently, tell me and we'll work it out.

Found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, please tell me by phone, text or email. I'll reply within 2 business days, fix it as soon as I can, and help you another way in the meantime.
