Pick a map style

Preview only. This page isn't linked anywhere and search engines are told to skip it. Tell me the letter you want.

ACurrent map

Hand-drawn dots and simplified highways.

  • Matches the site, loads instantly, nothing from Google
  • Busy, and the roads are not their real shapes

BClean rings

No roads. Rings show free (5 mi), 10 mi and 20 mi so people see the cost at a glance.

  • Simplest to read, nothing from outside servers
  • Not a real map, fewer town names

CReal map, dark

Real streets (OpenStreetMap) tinted to match the site. Green circle is the free 5 miles. Tap a dot for its price. Pinch to zoom.

  • Real roads and streets, zoom in to any street
  • Loads map pictures from OpenStreetMap, so the privacy policy gets one line

DReal map, light

Same as C in normal map colors.

  • Looks like a normal map app
  • Bright white box on a dark site

EGoogle Maps

A plain Google Maps window centered on Peabody.

  • Everyone knows it, with directions and Street View
  • Can't draw the free circle or prices without a paid Google key, and adds Google cookies

My pick: C. Real streets so people trust the distances, the free circle and prices still show on tap, and it matches the dark site. Whatever you pick, the town list and the "type your town or ZIP" checker stay the same.
