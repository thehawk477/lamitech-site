    Buy & sell

    Buy and sell.

    I buy broken consoles and controllers, and I sell the ones I've fixed.

    Sell me your broken one

    PS5, Xbox Series X|S and their controllers. Drift, dead buttons, overheating, won't power on. Send the model and a video for an offer. Cash or Zelle.

    Rules for selling to me
    1. You must be 18 or older. Under 18? A parent or guardian has to be there and make the sale.
    2. Show a valid photo ID. I write down your name, contact info, the device, its serial number, the price and the date. I don't keep a copy of your ID.
    3. It has to be yours. No stolen, borrowed or unpaid financed devices.
    4. Remove your account first. Sign out of PlayStation Network or Xbox. I can't buy a device that's banned or locked to an account.
    5. Tell me every problem. My offer is based on what you tell me. If I find something you didn't mention, I'll make a new offer before paying, and you can say no.
    6. I check it, then I pay. Cash or Zelle, right there.
    7. I can say no to any deal. I won't buy anything I think is stolen, and I may report it to the police.

    FAQ

    Questions.

    Can I figure out the problem myself first?

    Yes. Pick your device under Get a quote and tap the 1-minute check. It walks you through the first steps PlayStation and Xbox support recommend. Still broken? Text me from there.

    How long does a repair take?

    Controllers are often done the same day, sometimes in under an hour. Consoles usually take 1 to 2 days. I'll confirm your time when I quote, and text you if anything changes.

    When can I reach you?

    Every day, 7 AM to 10 PM. Text works best. You can text anytime, and I'll reply once I'm open.

    Is there a guarantee?

    Yes, 30 days on the repair I did, starting the day you get your device back. If the same problem comes back, text me and I fix it free. Your receipt helps, but if you lost it I can look up your repair by name and phone number. New damage, like drops or spills, isn't covered. It's on top of any rights you already have by law. Full guarantee terms.

    What if you can't fix it?

    You pay nothing: no repair charge and no travel fee. I put it back together the way it came and return it. If the device was already fragile, I'll warn you about any risk before I open it.

    Will I lose my games or saves?

    Your games and saves aren't part of the repair. I never need your passwords, and I don't look through your files. A hardware failure can still wipe data in rare cases, so back up anything important first.

    What should I bring or remove?

    Just the device. Take out any disc, and remove cases, skins or accessories that don't need fixing. Tell me about any earlier repairs, drops or spills.

    What parts do you use?

    New replacement parts that work like the originals, like Alps stick modules for DualSense stick drift. They may not be made by Sony or Microsoft.

    Do you fix water damage?

    Not right now. Liquid can cause corrosion that keeps spreading even after a device dries, so reliable repairs are hard to guarantee. A shop that specializes in liquid damage is your best bet.

    How do I pay?

    Cash or Zelle, when it's done and you've seen it working. Nothing up front. I'll never ask for your bank login, card number or Zelle password.
