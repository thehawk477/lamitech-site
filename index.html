FAQ

Questions people ask

How long does a repair take?

Most controller repairs are done within a few days. Console cleanings usually take a day or two. I'll give you a time when I give you the price.

What if you can't fix it?

Then you don't pay for the repair, and you get your device back the way you gave it to me.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. If the same problem comes back within 30 days, I'll fix it again for free.

Do you work on PS5 consoles?

I do PS5 controllers now. PS5 consoles use liquid metal instead of regular thermal paste, so I'm not taking PS5 cleanings yet. Text me and I'll tell you what I can do.

How do I pay?

Cash or Zelle, after the repair is done and you've seen it working.

Will you buy my broken console?

Probably. Text me the model, what's wrong, and a video. I'll make you an offer, and we meet locally.
