    Sorry, I can't fix water damage

    When water or any liquid gets inside a console or controller, it starts corroding the circuit board right away. The damage keeps spreading even after the device dries out, so a repair that seems to work often fails again days or weeks later.

    I don't want to charge you for a fix I can't stand behind, so I don't take water-damaged devices for repair.

    If it just got wet: unplug it, don't turn it on, and let it dry completely before trying it again.
