    FAQ

    Questions.

    How much does a repair cost?

    Prices start at what's listed above, parts included. Text me the model and problem and you'll get your exact price before any work.

    Can I figure out the problem myself first?

    Yes. Pick your device under Get a quote and tap the 1-minute check. It walks you through the first steps PlayStation and Xbox support recommend. Still broken? Text me from there.

    Is there a fee to check my device?

    No. Quotes are free. If I can't fix it, you don't pay.

    How long does a repair take?

    Controllers: a few days. Cleanings: a day or two. I'll give you a time with your quote.

    Do you pick up?

    Yes. Free pickup and drop-off within 10 miles of Peabody. Farther out, a small travel fee, quoted upfront. We can also meet somewhere public.

    I'm not on the North Shore. Can you help?

    Yes. Bring it by in Peabody, or text me for a travel quote.

    Is there a guarantee?

    Yes. Same problem within 30 days? Fixed free. New damage, like drops or spills, isn't covered.

    Will I lose my games or saves?

    Repairs don't touch your data, and I never need your passwords. Still, back up anything important first.

    What parts do you use?

    Quality replacement parts. I test every repair before it goes back to you.

    Do you fix stick drift?

    Yes. From $25 per controller, one stick or both. Calibrated and tested.

    Do you fix water damage?

    No. Corrosion keeps spreading after a device dries, so those repairs rarely last.

    How do I pay?

    Cash or Zelle, once it works.

    Will you buy my broken console?

    Probably. Text the model and a video for an offer.
