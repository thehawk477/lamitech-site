Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Lami Tech Repair is a small game controller and console repair business in Peabody, Massachusetts, owned and operated by Sajjad Lami. This page explains what information I collect and what I do with it.

Information you give me

When you call, text, email or message me, I get whatever you send: your name, phone number or email, details about your device, and any photos or videos. I use this only to give you a price, do the repair or purchase, and follow up about that job. If you buy from me or sell to me, I keep basic records of the sale for my own bookkeeping and taxes.

Information collected when you visit this website

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which buttons people tap, such as Call or Text. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects things like the pages you view, your general location (city level), your device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. I see this only as overall statistics, not as information about you by name. Google's use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard technical logs (like IP addresses) to run and protect the site.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell your information.
  • I don't share it with anyone, except the services listed above or when the law requires it.
  • I don't look through personal data on devices you bring me, beyond what's needed to test the repair.

Your choices

You can ask me to delete your messages and contact details at any time, unless I need to keep a sales record for taxes. Just call, text or email.
