Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Lami Tech Repair is a small game controller and console repair business in Peabody, Massachusetts, owned and operated by Sajjad Lami. This page explains what information I collect, why, who it's shared with, how long I keep it, and what choices you have. It covers this website (lamitechrepair.pages.dev) and the messages you send me.

Information you give me

When you call, text, email or message me, I get whatever you send: your name, phone number or email, details about your device, and any photos or videos. I use it only to give you a price, do the repair or purchase, and follow up about that job.

The "What needs fixing?" tool on this site doesn't send me anything by itself. It writes a message on your own device, and nothing reaches me unless you choose to send that text or email.

If you buy from me or sell to me, I keep basic records of the sale (date, item, amount and how it was paid) for my bookkeeping and taxes.

Information collected automatically on this website

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and understand how the site is used. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology and collects:

  • Pages viewed, how long you stay, and how you arrived (for example, from Google or Facebook)
  • General location (city level), device type, browser and operating system
  • Which buttons you tap, such as Call, Text, Email, review links and social links
  • In the "What needs fixing?" tool: which device and which problem category you pick (for example, "PS5" and "Fan stopped working"). Anything you type in the "Other" box is not sent.
  • What you type into the site's search bar, so I can see what people are looking for. Please don't type personal details like your phone number or address there.

I see this only as overall statistics, not as information tied to your name. Google processes it under its own policies. You can block cookies in your browser settings or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard technical logs (like IP addresses) to run and protect the site.

The site saves one small setting in your browser if you press "Pause animation," so the animation stays paused next time. It isn't sent anywhere.

Do Not Track

Browsers' "Do Not Track" signals don't have one agreed meaning, and this site doesn't change its behavior when it receives one. You can still block or opt out of analytics as described above.

Who I share information with

  • I don't sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • It's handled by the services this business runs on: Google (Analytics, Gmail and Google Voice), Cloudflare (website hosting), and the platforms you choose to contact me through, like Facebook or Yelp.
  • I'll share information only if the law requires it, for example a valid court order.

How long I keep it

  • Messages and contact details: as long as needed to finish your job and answer follow-up questions, then deleted when you ask or when I no longer need them.
  • Sales records: as long as tax law requires, which is generally several years.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google Analytics under its data retention settings, which this site sets to the shortest standard period available.

How I protect it

The website uses HTTPS encryption. I use strong passwords and two-step verification on the business accounts. I don't look through personal data on devices you bring me, beyond what's needed to test the repair, and I don't copy anything off them.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete the messages and contact details I have from you at any time. Some sales records may need to be kept for taxes. Just call, text or email and I'll take care of it.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle information, I'll update this page and the effective date at the top. Big changes will also be noted on the home page.
