Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Privacy at a glance.

  • I collect only what I need to fix or buy your device.
  • I don't sell your information. Ever.
  • I don't look through the data on your device.
  • You can ask me to delete your information at any time.

Lami Tech Repair is a one-person repair business in Peabody, Massachusetts, run by Sajjad Lami. This policy explains how I handle your information on this website and when you contact me.

What is personal information?

Anything that identifies you, like your name, phone number, email, or photos you send. Information that can't identify you, like overall visitor counts, isn't personal information.

Your privacy rights

You can ask me to:

  • See the information I have about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it
  • Stop contacting you

Text, call or email me and I'll take care of it. Some sales records must be kept for taxes.

What I collect

When you contact me

Your name, phone number or email, device details, and any photos or videos you send.

When you buy or sell

The date, item, amount and payment method, for bookkeeping and taxes.

When you use this website

Google Analytics records pages viewed, general location (city), device and browser type, how you found the site, and which buttons you tap. It also records the device and problem you pick in the quote tool, and what you type in the search bar. Please don't type personal details into the search bar. Anything you type in the quote tool's "Other" box stays on your device.

The quote tool doesn't send me anything. It writes a message on your device, and nothing reaches me unless you send it.

How I use it

  • To give you a price and do the work
  • To follow up about your repair or sale
  • To keep records the law requires
  • To see which parts of the website help people, so I can improve it

I don't use your information for advertising.

Who I share it with

Only the services that run this business: Google (Analytics, Gmail and Google Voice), Cloudflare (website hosting), and any platform you choose to contact me on, like Facebook or Yelp. I'll share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

How I protect it

This site uses HTTPS encryption. I use strong passwords and two-step verification on business accounts. I don't access, copy or keep data from devices you bring me.

How long I keep it

  • Messages and contact details: until your job is finished and questions are answered, or until you ask me to delete them.
  • Sales records: as long as tax law requires, generally several years.
  • Website analytics: 2 months, the shortest setting Google Analytics offers.

Cookies and Do Not Track

Google Analytics uses cookies. You can block them in your browser or use Google's opt-out add-on. Learn how Google uses this data here.

If you pause the animation, your browser saves that choice. It isn't sent anywhere.

There's no agreed standard for "Do Not Track" signals, so this site doesn't respond to them. You can still opt out as described above.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13. I don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted on the home page.
