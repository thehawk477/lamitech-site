Which model do I have?

Not sure which console or controller you have? Here's how to tell. You can also just pick "Other" or skip the model, and I'll figure it out from a photo.

  • PlayStation
  • Xbox
  • PlayStation controller
  • Xbox controller

PlayStation

PS5 (original)

The biggest one. White side panels that curve out like wings, with a black middle. Model number starts with CFI-10, CFI-11 or CFI-12.

PS5 Slim

Smaller. Each white side is split into a top panel and a bottom panel. Model number starts with CFI-20.

PS5 Pro

Three black stripes run across the middle of the white side panels. Model number starts with CFI-70.

The model number is on the bottom of the console when it's standing upright (PlayStation Support (opens in a new tab)). PS4 or older? Pick "Other" and type it in.

Xbox

Xbox Series X

A tall black tower, with green-tinted vents on top and a disc drive on the front.

Xbox Series S

Much smaller, usually white (some are black), with one big round black vent on the side. No disc drive.

Sizes for both are on Xbox's compare page (opens in a new tab). Xbox One, One S, One X or 360? Pick "Other" and type it in.

PlayStation controller

DualSense

The standard PS5 controller, with a touchpad in the middle.

DualSense Edge

Has two buttons on the back, stick caps that pop off, and small extra buttons under the sticks.

A PS4 controller has a light bar on the top edge and a smaller touchpad. For that one, pick "Other."

Xbox controller

Series X|S controller

Has a small Share button in the middle, below the Xbox button, and a USB-C port on top.

Xbox One controller

Looks almost the same, but has no Share button.

Elite controller

Rubber grips and paddles on the back.

Xbox 360 or something else? Pick "Other."
