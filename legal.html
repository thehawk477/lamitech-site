Terms of Service

Effective November 19, 2026

The short version.

  • Price before work.You get a quote first. Nothing extra without your OK.
  • No fix, no fee.If I can't fix it, you don't pay for the repair.
  • 30-day guarantee.Same problem comes back? Fixed again, free.
  • Pay when it works.Cash or Zelle, after you see it working.

These terms cover repairs, purchases and sales with Lami Tech Repair, a business owned and operated by Sajjad Lami in Peabody, Massachusetts ("I", "me"). By booking a repair, or buying or selling a device with me, you agree to them. Nothing here limits rights you have under Massachusetts law.

Need these terms in another format, like large print or read aloud over the phone? Call, text or email me and I'll get it to you.

1. Quotes and prices

  • Prices on this site are starting prices and include parts. A "+" after a price means it can be higher depending on the device and the problem.
  • You always get your exact price before any work starts.
  • Every quote is based on what you describe. If I open your device and find a different or bigger problem, I'll tell you the new price first. Nothing extra happens without your OK.
  • Stick drift is priced per controller, whether one stick or both need replacing.
  • Sales tax is added where the law requires it.
  • Quotes are based on current parts prices and are good for 14 days.
  • I use new replacement parts that work like the originals. They may not be made or sold by Sony or Microsoft.
  • I only install parts I supply. I don't install parts customers bring.

Example: I quote $70 to clean your PS5. Once it's open, I find a broken fan. I text you the new price. If you say no, I close it up and you pay nothing.

2. Before you hand over your device

  • Back up anything you care about. Your games, saves and files aren't part of the repair, and I don't access them. In rare cases a hardware failure or an unexpected problem during a repair can still cause data loss.
  • Remove discs, cases, cards and accessories you don't need repaired. I'm not responsible for items left with a device unless we agreed I'd keep them.
  • Tell me about any earlier repairs, drops or liquid spills.
  • Some devices are already fragile, like brittle ribbon cables, worn clips or a damaged board. Opening them can make an existing problem worse. If I see a risk like that, I'll tell you before I start, and you decide.
  • If I find signs of earlier liquid damage when I open a device, I'll stop and tell you before going further.
  • I take photos when I receive a device, so we both know its condition. I may share photos of my repair work, like the inside of a console, but never anything that shows your name, serial number or personal information.

3. Repairs I don't take

  • Water or liquid damage. I don't take these jobs right now. Corrosion can keep spreading after a device dries, so these repairs rarely last, and I only take jobs I can stand behind.
  • Devices that are banned, locked to someone else's account, or reported stolen.
  • Services marked "Coming soon", like phone repair and soldering or board repair (HDMI and charging ports). I don't take these jobs until they're listed with a price.
  • Devices that aren't on my price list, like older consoles, until I've looked at them and told you I can fix them.
  • Any job I don't believe I can do reliably. I'll tell you upfront.

4. If I can't fix it

You don't pay for the repair, and your device comes back as you gave it to me. If opening it made an existing problem worse, like a part that was already cracked or corroded, I'll tell you exactly what changed before I return it.

5. Payment

You pay by cash or Zelle when your device is returned and you've seen it working. Zelle payments can't be reversed, so check the amount before you send it. I never pay for reviews or give discounts in exchange for them. You can leave any review you want.

6. 30-day guarantee

If the same problem I fixed comes back within 30 days, I'll fix it again for free. The guarantee covers the repair I did and the parts I replaced.

After every repair, I text or email you a receipt. Keep it, because it's your guarantee record. To use the guarantee, text me with the receipt.

This guarantee is my own promise. It's in addition to any rights you have under Massachusetts law, and it doesn't replace them. Opening a device can affect a manufacturer's warranty, so if yours is still under a Sony or Microsoft warranty, check with them first.

The guarantee doesn't cover:

  • New problems unrelated to my repair
  • Drops, cracks, liquid or other damage after you get your device back
  • Devices opened or repaired by someone else after me
  • Parts I didn't replace, like the other stick on a controller where I replaced one
  • Other parts that were already wearing out and fail on their own, unrelated to my repair
  • Earlier liquid damage, even if it wasn't visible when you gave me the device
  • Misuse, overheating from blocked vents, or changes made to the device after my repair

Example: I fix a mushy bumper and it goes mushy again three weeks later. I fix it free. If the controller gets dropped and the shell cracks, that's not covered.

7. Pickup, drop-off and travel

  • Pickup, drop-off and meetups are free within 5 miles of Peabody (driving distance).
  • Farther than 5 miles, the travel fee is $0.75 per mile past the first 5 miles, counted one way from Peabody to you, for each trip. Pickup only is one trip. Pickup and drop-off are two trips, so you pay the fee twice. The pickup cost check on my website gives an estimate for any Massachusetts town or ZIP code. I'll confirm the exact fee before I come, and you can say no.
  • To skip the fee, drop your device off to me in Peabody, or meet me somewhere public within 5 miles of Peabody. Both are free.
  • You don't have to live on the North Shore. Anyone is welcome.
  • I may reschedule or cancel a pickup or meetup if I don't feel it's safe.
  • Please collect your device within 30 days after I tell you it's ready.

If you don't collect your device

  • I'll text and email you when it's ready, and again at 30 and 60 days.
  • I'll keep your device safe for at least 90 days.
  • After 90 days with no reply, I'll send a final written notice to the phone and email you gave me.
  • If I still can't reach you, I'll only handle the device as Massachusetts law allows. I don't automatically become the owner of your device.

Example: You live in Salem (ZIP 01970), about 4 miles away. Pickup, drop-off and meetups are free. You live in Newburyport, about 25 miles away. That's 20 miles past the free area, so about $15 for pickup only, or about $30 for pickup and drop-off. I confirm the fee first. Or meet me within 5 miles of Peabody, and it's free.

8. Help for customers with disabilities

If you have a disability and need something done differently, like communicating only by text or email, extra time, or written quotes, tell me. I'll work with you to make it happen.

9. Selling a device to me

  • You must be 18 or older. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian has to be there and make the sale.
  • You must show a valid photo ID. I don't keep a copy of it.
  • You must own the device and have the right to sell it. It can't be stolen, borrowed, or financed and not paid off.
  • Sign out of and remove your PlayStation Network or Xbox account first. I can't buy a device that's banned or locked to an account.
  • Describe its problems honestly. My offer is based on what you tell me and show me. If I find a problem you didn't mention, I'll make a new offer before paying, and you can say no.
  • I check the device before I pay. Payment is cash or Zelle.
  • I keep a record of each purchase: the date, the device, its serial number, the price, and the seller's name and contact information (and the parent or guardian's, for a seller under 18).
  • I can turn down any purchase. I won't buy a device I believe is stolen, and I may report it to the police.
  • If a device you sold me turns out to be stolen or not yours to sell, you agree to pay back what I paid, and I'll cooperate with the police.

10. Buying a device from me

  • Every device I sell is tested before sale. Its condition and any flaws are listed with the price.
  • The listed price is the price. Payment is cash or Zelle at pickup, and you can test the device before you pay.
  • Every device is factory reset, with no one else's account or data on it.
  • It comes with the same 30-day guarantee as my repairs: if it stops working because of a defect within 30 days of the day you get it, I fix it free, and if I can't fix it, I refund what you paid.
  • The guarantee doesn't cover drops, cracks, liquid, or a device opened or repaired by someone else after the sale.
  • This guarantee is in addition to any rights you have under Massachusetts law.

11. Responsibility

I'm careful with every device. If my work causes a problem, I'll fix it again for free, or refund what you paid for that repair. If your device is lost or damaged while it's with me because of my mistake, I'll repair that damage for free or pay you its fair market value, meaning what the same model in similar condition sells for used, based on its condition right before it was lost or damaged.

To the extent the law allows, I'm not responsible for data lost because of a failure I didn't cause, for indirect losses like missed game time, or for console bans, account problems, game licenses, or software and online services run by Sony or Microsoft. Nothing in these terms limits my responsibility for damage caused by my own carelessness, or anything else the law doesn't let me exclude. This doesn't limit any rights you have under Massachusetts consumer protection law.

12. This website

The website is provided for information. Prices and services can change. If the site and a written quote from me disagree, the written quote wins.

Sending a message through the contact form doesn't book a repair. A repair is booked once we agree on the price and a time.

The self-check

  • The 1-minute self-check shares basic first steps based on PlayStation and Xbox support guides. It's general help, not a diagnosis. Only a hands-on check confirms what's wrong.
  • Following the steps is your choice, and only do them as written. Don't open your console or controller yourself. That can void the manufacturer's warranty and cause damage.
  • Links to PlayStation, Xbox and other sites go to pages I don't run. Their own terms apply there.

Example: The self-check says your PS5 sounds like dust buildup. You vacuum the vents and it's still loud. Text me and I'll quote a cleaning. The self-check's guess doesn't set the price. My quote does.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Privacy at a glance.

  • Only what I need.Just enough to fix or buy your device.
  • Never sold.I don't sell your information. Ever.
  • Your files stay yours.I don't look through the data on your device.
  • Delete anytime.Ask, and I'll delete it, except records the law makes me keep.

Lami Tech Repair is a one-person repair business in Peabody, Massachusetts, run by Sajjad Lami. This policy explains how I handle your information on this website and when you contact me.

What is personal information?

Anything that identifies you, like your name, phone number, email, or photos you send. Information that can't identify you, like overall visitor counts, isn't personal information. Website analytics sits in between: Google Analytics uses cookies and an anonymous ID to tell visits apart, which I treat with the same care.

Your privacy rights

You can ask me to:

  • See the information I have about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it
  • Stop contacting you

Text, call or email me and I'll take care of it. Some sales records must be kept for taxes.

What I collect

When you contact me

Your name, phone number or email, device details, and any photos or videos you send.

When you use the contact form

Your name if you give it, your email or phone number (only one is needed), and your message. The form sends it to my email inbox. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

When you book a pickup

The address or meeting spot you give me, so I can pick up and return your device. I use it only for that job.

When I receive your device

Photos of your device's condition, and a receipt I text or email you after the repair. If I share photos of my repair work, they never show your name, serial number or personal information.

When you ask to be notified

If you text me to hear when phone repair or soldering starts, I keep your number only to send you that one update. Reply STOP or ask me and I'll remove it.

When you buy or sell

The date, item, amount and payment method, for bookkeeping and taxes. When I buy a device from you, I also record its serial number and your name and contact information, and a parent or guardian's name and contact information if you're under 18. I check your photo ID, but I don't keep a copy.

Payments

I take cash or Zelle. Zelle goes through your bank, and I only see the name and amount your bank shows me. I never ask for, collect or store bank logins, card numbers or Zelle passwords. If someone claiming to be me asks for them, it isn't me.

When you use this website

Google Analytics records pages viewed, general location (city), device and browser type, how you found the site, and which buttons you tap. It also records the device and problem you pick in the quote tool, which symptom you pick in the self-check, what you type in the search bar, and whether a pickup cost check came out free, with a fee, on an island, or not found. It doesn't record the town or ZIP code you check. Please don't type personal details into the search bar.

Example: You search "PS5 overheating". Analytics records that a visitor searched that phrase. It doesn't record your name or number.

Anything you type in the quote tool's "Other" box stays on your device. The same goes for the town or ZIP code you type or tap in the pickup cost check. It's worked out on your device and never sent to me.

The quote tool doesn't send me anything. It writes a message on your device, including how it gets to me and back, your town or ZIP if I'm picking up or dropping off, and the day and time. The travel fee note in the quote tool is worked out on your device too. Nothing reaches me unless you send it.

How I use it

  • To give you a price and do the work
  • To follow up about your repair or sale
  • To keep records the law requires
  • To see which parts of the website help people, so I can improve it

I don't use your information for advertising.

Who I share it with

Only the services that run this business: Google (Analytics, Gmail and Google Voice), Cloudflare (website hosting), FormSubmit (formsubmit.co, which delivers contact form messages to my Gmail), and any service you choose to contact me through, like your email provider, Facebook, Instagram, Yelp or Nextdoor. Each of these services handles information under its own privacy policy and terms. Links in the self-check open PlayStation, Xbox or other support sites, which have their own privacy policies. I'll share information with authorities only when the law requires it. The font on this site is stored on my own website, so loading a page doesn't contact any font service.

How I protect it

This site uses HTTPS encryption. I use strong passwords on business accounts. I don't browse, copy or keep the files, photos, messages or other data stored on devices you bring me. The only photos I take are of the device itself, to record its condition and, sometimes, to show my repair work. Those never show your name, serial number or personal information.

How long I keep it

  • "Get notified" numbers: until I send the update, or until you ask me to remove them.
  • Pickup addresses: until your device is returned and your job is finished, then deleted.
  • Photos of your device's condition: until the 30-day guarantee ends, then deleted.
  • Receipts: as long as tax law requires, like other sales records.
  • Contact form messages: same as messages and contact details, below.
  • Purchase records for devices I buy: as long as tax law and any local rules require.
  • Messages and contact details: until your job is finished and questions are answered, or until you ask me to delete them.
  • Sales records: as long as tax law requires, generally several years.
  • Website analytics: visit-level data is kept for 2 months, the shortest setting Google Analytics offers. Google keeps overall totals, like monthly visitor counts, under its own policy.

Cookies and Do Not Track

Google Analytics uses cookies and an anonymous ID to tell visits apart. You can block them in your browser or use Google's opt-out add-on. Learn how Google uses this data here.

If you pause the animation, your browser saves that choice. It isn't sent anywhere. The menu and the top bar that fades when you scroll don't collect anything.

There's no agreed standard for "Do Not Track" signals, so this site doesn't respond to them. You can still opt out as described above.

Other formats

Need this policy in large print, read aloud over the phone, or explained another way? Call, text or email me.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13. I don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted on the home page.

Accessibility

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Accessibility policy

Lami Tech Repair is committed to making its website and services usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control, magnification or other assistive technology. Accessibility is checked every time the site is changed, and problems people report are treated as a priority.

Conformance status

As of November 19, 2026, the pages listed below conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, published by the W3C at https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/.

  • Pages covered: https://lamitechrepair.pages.dev/ (home page, including the search bar, the "What needs fixing?" tool and self-check, the reviews carousel, the pickup map and cost check, the Buy and sell section with its rules, and the contact form), /legal.html (Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Accessibility), the Which model page (/which-model.html) and the town pages (/salem-ma.html, /danvers-ma.html, /beverly-ma.html, /lynn-ma.html, /marblehead-ma.html, /swampscott-ma.html, /lynnfield-ma.html and /saugus-ma.html)
  • Technologies this conformance relies on: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and WAI-ARIA
  • How it was checked: a self-evaluation by the site owner using the axe-core automated testing engine for WCAG 2.2 A and AA, plus manual checks: keyboard-only use, visible focus, focus not hidden by the sticky menu or phone bar, target sizes, reflow at 320 pixels wide, 200% text size, increased text spacing, reduced motion and high-contrast modes, the Menu button (open, close with Esc, focus returns), the top bar that fades on scroll, the reviews carousel (arrow buttons, keyboard scrolling, Read more), the contact form (labels, required fields, error messages and an error summary) and the pickup cost check (town buttons, results read out by screen readers)
  • Not covered: websites this site links to, such as Google, Facebook, Instagram, Yelp and Apple Maps, and the phone, text and email apps on your own device

What's built in

  • Strong color contrast, and information is never shown by color alone.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Form choices have labels and instructions, and clear error messages appear if something is missing.
  • Pop-up windows can be closed with the Esc key, and focus returns to where you were.
  • Images and icons have text descriptions, or are hidden from screen readers when they're only decoration.
  • The moving background at the top can be paused, and it stays still if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Buttons and links are large enough to tap, and the page works with larger text, extra spacing and narrow screens.
  • The site adjusts for high contrast modes, including Windows Contrast Themes.
  • The self-check in the quote tool is fully keyboard friendly. Focus moves to each new step, every option is a real button, and source links say they open in a new tab.
  • The pickup map shows real town borders, with each town colored by its pickup cost. It has a full text description that lists every town by price, so it doesn't rely on color. Every town on the map is also a button in the town list, with its price written next to its name, so you can check a town by keyboard or screen reader. The cost result is read out when it appears.
  • In Buy and sell, the rules open in place with a tap. The "I agree" box has a label, and if it isn't checked, a message says why and moves focus to the box.
  • The contact form has a visible label on every field, says which fields are needed, and lists any errors at the top with links to each field. You only need an email or a phone number, not both.
  • The reviews can be scrolled with arrow buttons, a keyboard or a swipe. Nothing moves on its own. "Read more" shows a full review in place.
  • On every screen size, a Menu button lists every section. It opens and closes with a keyboard, and Esc closes it.
  • The top bar fades out when you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up, hover, tap the top of the screen or move keyboard focus to it. If your device is set to reduce motion or increase contrast, it never fades.
  • Prices are in a real table with column headers, so screen readers read each price with its repair.
